📌 Biography

I'm Sepehr Ghaseminejad Liasi and I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from K.N.Toosi University of Technology, where I studied from 2019 to 2023. I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Data Science at the Tehran Institute of Advanced Sciences (TEIAS).
This webpage provides a brief introduction to my background, academic journey, and primary areas of interest. You can also explore my university and professional experiences through the Projects and Work Experience sections. If you would like to get in touch, please feel free to use the contact information available on the Contact page.
Throughout my academic career in high school, I have participated in both the Mathematics Olympiad and the Informatics Olympiad, successfully advancing to the first and second stages and ranking among the top 100 students nationwide.
In addition, I competed in the International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC) in 2020 and 2022. Together with my teammates, I qualified for the regional contests on both occasions, achieving 17th and 13th place respectively among more than one hundred teams representing leading universities.
